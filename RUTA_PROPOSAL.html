The Problem

03

The Solution

RUTA combines local PUJ/PUV route data, interactive maps, and GPS tracking. Unlike apps that only give directions before a trip, it stays with you through the whole commute.

1

Enter where you are and where you're going, then see the available routes.

2

Follow boarding points, transfers, and landmarks, step-by-step.

3

Live GPS tracking tells you when your stop is near.

06

Objectives

Our goal: improve access to local transportation information and help Filipino commuters navigate unfamiliar routes.

  1. 01Identify PUJ and PUV routes from start point to destination.
  2. 02Give step-by-step directions with boarding, drop-off and transfers.
  3. 03Track journey progress with GPS.
  4. 04Alert users as they approach their stops.
  5. 05Open up local transport information through a friendly app.
  6. 06Reduce confusion on unfamiliar routes.
07

Who it's for

Primary users

  • College and university students commuting between home, campus and nearby places.
  • First-time commuters new to local routes.
  • Daily commuters who rely on PUJs and PUVs.

Also helps

  • Employees and workers
  • Tourists and visitors
  • Local residents

Prototype scope

In the prototype

Selected, verified PUJ and PUV routes in Batangas City: destination search, a limited route database, route visualization, step-by-step instructions, GPS monitoring and stop notifications.

Comes later

Live vehicle tracking and wider route coverage.
